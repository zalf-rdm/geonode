{% trans "About the BonaRes Repository" %}

{% trans "The BonaRes Repository is a research data repository for soil and agricultural research data. It enables researchers to publish, discover, access, and reuse research datasets." %}

{% trans "The repository is open to researchers from ZALF and other research institutions, supporting the long-term availability and reuse of research data in accordance with the FAIR principles." %}

{% trans "Our History" %}

  • {% trans "Originally" %} {% trans "Established as part of the BonaRes research initiative to manage and publish research data generated within the project." %}
  • 2017 {% trans "Opened to researchers beyond the BonaRes initiative, allowing the publication of external research datasets." %}
  • 2024 {% trans "ZALF committed to the long-term operation of the BonaRes Repository." %}
  • {% trans "Today" %} {% trans "An open research data repository for soil and agricultural research in Germany and beyond." %}

{% trans "Operation and Infrastructure" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The BonaRes Repository is operated and maintained by the Leibniz Centre for Agricultural Landscape Research (ZALF) in Müncheberg, Germany. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} It is based on GeoNode, an open-source platform for managing, publishing, and sharing geospatial data. ZALF is responsible for the technical operation and continued development of the repository. {% endblocktrans %}
